Executive Interview Etiquette & Dressing Guide

👔

Professional Dressing Standard

MNC & Tech Tier-1 Norms

  • Virtual Camera Presence: Wear solid colors (navy, charcoal, pastel blue, crisp white). Avoid thin stripes or houndstooth that cause moiré camera flicker.
  • Formal / Smart Casual: Tailored collared shirts, blazers, or business blouses. Ensure clean grooming and well-fitted attire.
  • Lighting & Posture: Position primary lighting source in front of you. Keep camera at eye level with 30% headspace.

🗣️

Voice Tone & Non-Verbal Cues

Confidence & Articulation

  • Direct Camera Eye-Contact: Look directly at your camera lens when speaking, not just the screen, to project genuine confidence.
  • The 2-Second Pause Rule: Take a 2-second thoughtful breath before answering complex questions rather than rushing or using filler sounds ("um", "like").
  • Pacing & Modulation: Speak at ~140 words per minute. Enunciate technical keywords clearly and concisely.
🌐 Coursera Voice & Posture ↗ 🌐 Toastmasters Speech Tips ↗
🎯

The STAR Storytelling Model

Behavioral & Architecture Scenarios

  • Situation (15%): Briefly set the project context and high-stakes problem.
  • Task (15%): State the objective and technical constraint you needed to solve.
  • Action (50%): Highlight *your* specific engineering actions, tools, and algorithms.
  • Result (20%): Deliver quantifiable business metrics (e.g., "Reduced latency by 45%").
